# Workflow recipes

Adapt one recipe, test it manually, then decide whether it should recur. These are prompts, not pre-enabled automations.

## Reading brief

“Help me build a weekly reading brief on [topic]. Use no more than five sources, include why each matters, and distinguish evidence from opinion. First produce one sample for me to review.”

After you like the sample: “Every Friday at 3 PM [timezone], create that reading brief here.”

## Project check-in

“Using only this conversation and its files, summarize the current goal, what is complete, the next three actions, and anything blocked. Do not invent progress.”

## Writing review

“@Reviewer check this draft for unsupported claims, ambiguity and unnecessary repetition. Give the three most useful corrections, then let me choose what to change.”

## Connected calendar summary

“Read tomorrow’s calendar from my connected account. Summarize the schedule, overlapping events and preparation I should consider. Do not create, cancel or send anything.”

Test the connection and result before making it recurring.

## Browser task with a human checkpoint

“Find the options that match these constraints and show the evidence. Stop when you need me to sign in, confirm private details or approve an external action. Preserve your place so we can resume.”

A site’s availability, authentication or verification flow can still require your involvement. Do not promise that switching providers removes those requirements.
